# Logo Brief

## Brand Name
BAPI - Benue AgriPrice Insight

## Brand Meaning
The brand should communicate agricultural intelligence, grounded decision-making, and trust rooted in nature and local relevance.

## Visual Requirements
- Large, bold, highly legible wordmark: `BAPI`
- Elegant modern serif or refined display styling
- Stylized leaf or sprouting plant integrated with `B` or positioned near the wordmark
- Optional understated upward analytical cue within the plant form
- Faint grid or horizontal line motif to echo farm plots and data structure

## Color Rules
- Evergreen: `#0F3A2F`
- Jade: `#34C9A2`
- Mint: `#A1E8C8`
- Background compatibility: `#F8F7F2`

## Required Variants
- Primary horizontal
- Stacked
- Icon-only
- Monochrome

## Usage Locations
- Dashboard header
- Login page
- Presentation slides
- Final report cover
- Favicon / app icon

## Visual Tone
Premium, earthy, modern, trustworthy, calm, and academically credible. No neon, no glossy effects, and no noisy ornamentation.
