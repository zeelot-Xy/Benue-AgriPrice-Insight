# Supervisor Log

## Purpose
This document records supervisor interactions, approvals, refinements, and decisions that materially affect project scope, implementation, or academic write-up.

## Usage Notes
- Add a new row after every meaningful supervisor meeting or review.
- Record concrete decisions, not vague summaries.
- If scope or methodology changes, update the affected document after logging the decision here.

## Log Template
| Date | Phase | Discussion Summary | Decisions Made | Action Items | Documents to Update |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Phase X | Brief summary of discussion | Key approved decisions | Specific next steps | List affected files |
